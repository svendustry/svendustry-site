How It Works

From first call to a stocked machine.

The whole program is designed so that your part of it is one conversation. After that, it is our work.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Start with a no-obligation consultation. Tell us about your location — the kind of business it is, how many people move through it, and where a machine might live.

  2. 02

    We look it over

    We talk through whether vending makes sense for your space. If it does, we line up placement at no cost to you.

  3. 03

    We place and load the machine

    Stock-Well delivers the machine, positions it where it will actually get used, and loads it with a starting mix of drinks and snacks.

  4. 04

    We keep it going

    Regular restocking visits keep the shelves full, and we handle maintenance when the machine needs it. That is the “full-service” part.

Ready to start at step one?

It is just a conversation, and it costs nothing.
