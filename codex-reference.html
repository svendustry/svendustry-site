SEC 07
Safety

Never paste tokens, cookies, private keys, OAuth material, or passwords into Codex chat. Refer to secret locations, not values.

Use workspace-write by default. Avoid danger-full-access unless the task is externally sandboxed and you fully intend that risk.

Before model-routing, service, network, or auth changes: read current state, make a rollback backup, validate config, and do not restart services without approval.

Do not use these casually: git reset --hard, rm -rf, openclaw doctor --fix, openclaw update, public Tailscale Funnel, or unreviewed curl-to-shell commands.
