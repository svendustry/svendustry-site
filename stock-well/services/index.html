What We Do

One provider, the whole vending program.

Stock-Well is a full-service vending provider. You get a complete machine placement, and we keep it running — without buying a machine or hiring a vendor.

  1. 01

    Free machine placement

    Stock-Well supplies the vending machine and places it in a spot that works for your space. The machine itself is free — that is the point of the offer.

  2. 02

    Product stocking

    We load the machine with the kinds of drinks and snacks your people actually buy, and keep it stocked through regular restocking visits.

  3. 03

    Maintenance and repairs

    When a machine needs attention, we come out and take care of it. Ongoing upkeep is part of the service, not an add-on you track yourself.

  4. 04

    Ongoing service visits

    Because we serve Phoenix and the West Valley, our team can service your machine on a regular local schedule instead of shipping parts or waiting on a national vendor.
